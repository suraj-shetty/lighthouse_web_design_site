Layout

  • 375 — full-screen translucent overlay: the page beneath stays visible, blurred and darkened. Horizontal pager, each media fitted (contain) and centred. Round glass back button top-left, "n/N" pill top-right (only with more than one media). Video pages add a mute button above a full-width scrubber.
  • 768 — same overlay with margins round the media and visible previous / next buttons.
  • 1440 — lightbox over the page: close button top-right, counter top-left, previous / next at the sides, zoom toggle bottom-right; the video controls sit in a centred 720 bar.
  • Swipe (touch / trackpad) or use the arrows: every slide carries its own counter, so it is always right. Try the zoom toggle (1× ↔ 2×) and the pause and mute toggles.
  • States: image, video first, controls hidden (3 s after the last interaction on a video), single media (no counter), image loading (spinner), image error and video error (glyph only, no text, no retry).

Components used

  • Image gallery / Media viewer (fullscreen viewer, image count badge)
  • IconButton glass — back / close, previous, next, zoom, pause, mute
  • Progress bar (video scrubber)
  • Spinner

Motion

  • Open / close — --mediaview-open-dur
  • Paging — --tab-panel-* (native snap scroll in the mockup)
  • Double-tap / zoom toggle — --resize-dur
  • Mute / pause glyph — --icon-swap-*; controls fade — --reveal-*

Accessibility

  • Modal dialog labelled "Media viewer"; focus trapped; Esc closes; focus returns to the tapped media.
  • Back / close, previous / next, zoom, pause and mute are labelled; each slide is announced as "n of N".
  • The scrubber is a keyboard-operable slider with time values; controls reappear on focus even when auto-hidden.
  • Reduced motion: no zoom animation, no autoplay. Images need alt text (not in the data model).

Decisions to confirm

  • The backdrop is always dark in both themes; glass controls keep the theme's glass tokens.
  • Pause / play and the zoom toggle are web additions for keyboard and pointer users (the app has pinch, double-tap and autoplay only).
  • Video-off and broken-image glyphs are not in ICONS.md; Video01 and Image02 stand in.
  • From 768 the back arrow becomes a close (×) at the top-right and the counter moves to the top-left.
