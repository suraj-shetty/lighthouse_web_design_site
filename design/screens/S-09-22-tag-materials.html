Layout

  • 375 — full screen: the image at its real aspect ratio, a round marker at each tagged point; header overlay with close (left) and SAVE (right). No tags: a small glass hint card top-left. With tags: a bottom panel with the heading, helper line and a horizontal row of product cards (thumbnail, brand chip, title, Price + amount, remove badge on the corner).
  • 768 — modal; image constrained to the viewport, wider card row.
  • 1440 — modal media view: image on the left, "Products Used" as a right-hand list panel.
  • States: default (3 tags), empty (hint card, no panel), loading (shimmer until the image resolves). Error does not apply — no request is made here.
  • Tapping anywhere on the image — including on a marker — records the point and opens S-09-23; a returned product adds a marker and a card.

Components used

  • Full-screen / modal media view · IconButton glass (close) · Button filled S (SAVE)
  • Tag marker · glass hint card · zoom control
  • Product card (compact) · Tag (brand chip) · remove badge · Skeleton

Motion

  • Open — --mediaview-open-dur; product picker — --page-slide-* (desktop --modal-*)
  • New marker — --badge-pop-*; remove — --press-*

Accessibility

  • The image has alt text. The "Products Used" list is the accessible representation of the markers; every card has a "Remove" button.
  • Keyboard: the image is one focusable control; arrow keys move a crosshair and Enter opens the product picker for that point.
  • Zoom in / out buttons are keyboard reachable (the app uses pinch, 1×–5×). The tag count heading is a polite live region.

Decisions to confirm

  • Keyboard tagging (crosshair) and the zoom buttons are web additions — not in the app; confirm the pattern.
  • Markers use --success-default with a white ring. Background is --bg-default (the app uses pure black in dark mode).
  • Close discards unsaved tags without confirmation (Q-67). Cards are not links to the marketplace (Q-62).
