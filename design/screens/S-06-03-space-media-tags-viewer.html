Layout

  • 375 — full screen on the page background. Image mode: the image centred at its own aspect ratio with product-tag dots on it, round glass back button top-left and, when the image has tags, a bottom panel with rounded top corners: "Products Used (n)" over a horizontal strip of product cards (thumbnail · brand tag · title · "Price" + value), each about 62% of the width.
  • 768 — large modal, same arrangement; cards are a fixed 280 wide so several are visible.
  • 1440 — lightbox: image with dots on the left, the product list as a 360 side column on the right with the cards stacked; back top-left; zoom toggle bottom-right of the image.
  • Tap a dot: it enlarges and inverts, its product card gets the success outline and moves to the start of the strip. Tap it again to clear. Cards are not tappable. The viewer is read-only.
  • Video mode: the clip fills the stage letterboxed with pause, scrubber and mute; no panel. Image without tags: image only.
  • Loading: flat grey placeholder (image) or spinner (video). Error: broken-image glyph on grey, video-off glyph, glyph tile for a missing product thumbnail.

Components used

  • Image gallery / Media viewer (fullscreen)
  • IconButton glass — back, zoom, pause, mute
  • Static product panel (bottom panel / side column)
  • Product card, compact, with selected outline · Tag / Label (brand)
  • Product-tag dot (button with pressed state)
  • Progress bar (scrubber) · Spinner

Motion

  • Open / close — --mediaview-open-dur
  • Dot and card selection — --select-check-*
  • Strip scroll to the card — --tab-panel-*
  • Zoom — --resize-dur; mute / pause glyph — --icon-swap-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog labelled by the space ("Living Room media"); Back labelled; Esc closes; focus returns to the media that opened it.
  • Each dot is a button named "Product tag: {title}" with pressed state, a 44 × 44 hit area and a logical tab order.
  • The product strip is a list; the selected card is marked by outline weight and aria-current, not colour alone.
  • Zoom is available from the keyboard through the zoom toggle; dots keep their on-screen size while zoomed; reduced motion removes the animated strip scroll.

Decisions to confirm

  • Tag dot and selected-card green use --success-default (the app hard-codes a green, Q-33).
  • Card radius 14 in the app → --radius-16.
  • The zoom toggle (1× ↔ 2×) and pause are web additions; the app zooms 1×–5× by pinch.
  • Glyphs not in ICONS.md: broken-image → Image02, video-off → Video01.
  • In the mockup more than one dot can be pressed at once; in the product only one tag is selected at a time.
