Additional actions for this product.

Layout

  • 375 — content-height bottom sheet: handle, title, subtitle, two rows (icon, label, round arrow) with a hairline.
  • 768 / 1440 — popover menu anchored to the "…" button.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / popover menu
  • Menu row (icon, label, trailing round arrow)
  • Toast (neutral, share fallback)

Motion

  • Open / close — --panel-*
  • Report replaces this sheet with S-10-15 — --panel-* / --modal-*
  • Row press — --press-*

Accessibility

  • Menu with two items; arrow keys move, Esc closes, focus returns to the "…" button ("More actions").
  • Trailing arrows are decorative.

Decisions to confirm

  • Share on web: the browser's native share with "{title}\n\n{link}"; where that is unavailable the link is copied and the toast "Link copied" shows (same wording as the feed).
  • Glyphs: the app uses Alert02 (Report) and Share06 (Share), which are not approved; flag-02 and share-03 are used.
  • A missing product id shows the toast "Report" / "Unable to report this product" or "Share" / "Unable to share this product".
