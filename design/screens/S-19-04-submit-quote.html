Layout

  • 375 — mirrors the app: full screen without the reseller tab bar. Header "Submit Quote", "Order {number}", close X. One column: order summary card → Price per unit → Quote Total Amount → Delivery Timeline → Quote Validity → Additional Charges (two tiles per row, amount field under them when ticked) → Notes → "CANCEL" and "SUBMIT QUOTE" side by side at the end of the form (they scroll with it).
  • 768 — same column; price / total and the two dates sit in pairs; three charge tiles in one row; the buttons move to a sticky bar.
  • 1440 — two columns: the form on the left, the order summary sticky on the right so quantity, MRP and the buyer's note stay in view while pricing.
  • Tick a charge tile to reveal its required amount field (works in the mockup). The date fields open the shared date picker (S-09-13); earliest date is today.
  • "filled" shows the total worked out from unit price × quantity (6,200 × 24). Answered (Q-16): charges are not added to the total; they are sent separately.
  • On success the screen closes with no message and the lists refresh.

Components used

  • Top header · IconButton glass (close) · Product card (summary) · Tag · Key / value list
  • Text field (numeric, read-only, error) · Date trigger (Select) · Checkbox tile (Select card) · Textarea
  • Sticky CTA bar · Button translucent L (CANCEL) · Button filled L with loading (SUBMIT QUOTE, RETRY)
  • Skeleton · Empty state (load error) · Toast danger

Motion

  • Total update — --text-swap-*; charge tile — --select-check-*; amount field reveal — --reveal-*
  • Date picker — --panel-*; press — --press-*; toasts — --toast-*
  • Push / close — --page-slide-* under 768, --panel-* from 768; skeleton — --skel-*

Accessibility

  • Form region named "Submit Quote"; required fields carry required as well as the asterisk.
  • The total is read-only and updates through a polite live region.
  • Date fields are buttons that expose the chosen date as their value. Charges are checkboxes grouped under "Additional Charges (Optional)"; each amount field follows its group in focus order.
  • Validation: the app shows toasts only. Here the first failing field is also marked and described ("invalid" state), and announced.
  • Numeric fields use a numeric keyboard; the submit button announces "Submitting quote".
