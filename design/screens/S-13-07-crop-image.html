Layout

  • In-page cropper replacing the app's native crop screen.
  • 375 — full screen: title bar with close, dark canvas with the photo and the crop frame, zoom row with hint, Cancel and Crop side by side.
  • 768 / 1440 — centred 640 × 720 dialog with the same parts over the scrim.
  • States: profile (1:1 frame with circular mask; output up to 1080 × 1080) · cover (16:9 rectangle; up to 1600 × 900) · certificate ("Crop Image", free rectangle with corner handles) · loading (Crop shows a spinner while the image is produced). Aspect is locked for profile and cover.

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog (full on mobile)
  • IconButton glass (close) / plain (zoom out, zoom in)
  • Range slider (zoom 1× – 4×)
  • Button stroke L (Cancel) · Button filled L (Crop, loading)

Motion

  • Open / close — --modal-*
  • Button press — --press-*
  • Crop continues to the preview (S-13-08, or S-13-23 for certificates) — --modal-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title; focus trapped; Esc and Cancel abort with no change.
  • The crop frame is focusable: arrow keys move the photo, + / − zoom; the slider and both zoom buttons are labelled.
  • The dimmed area outside the frame is decorative; the result is confirmed again in the preview dialog.

Decisions to confirm

  • All labels except the three titles are new web copy (the app's come from the native plugin): "Cancel", "Crop", "Drag to reposition. Use the slider or + / − to zoom." (Q-115).
  • No rotate control: iOS hides it and the aspect is locked; Android's extra bottom controls are not carried over.
  • Certificate crop is shown with a free rectangle; its exact crop settings are in add_edit_certifications_bottomsheet.dart:407-421 and the flow belongs to S-13-21/22.
  • Output: JPEG quality 75 (profile, cover) / 80 (certificate), as the app.
