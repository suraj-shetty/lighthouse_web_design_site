Layout

  • 375 — full-height glass sheet: optional back arrow, title, subtitle, close top-right, rows (icon, name, chevron chip).
  • 768 / 1440 — side sheet from the right with the same pages.
  • Three flows: category → its subcategories; application → its subcategories; brand → the brand's categories (page 1), then the tapped category's subcategories (page 2, with back).
  • Loading (brand flow only): the sheet opens at once with 8 shimmer rows. If the brand has no categories the sheet closes itself and the brand's product list opens.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / side sheet · IconButton glass (close) · IconButton plain (back)
  • Menu row with leading glyph and trailing glass chevron chip
  • Skeleton rows

Motion

  • Open / close — --panel-*
  • Page 1 ⇄ page 2 — --page-slide-* (crossfade only under reduced motion)
  • Skeleton — --skel-*, --shimmer-*; row press — --press-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog labelled by its title; focus trapped and returned to the tile that opened it.
  • On a page change focus moves to the new title and the change is announced.
  • Back and close buttons are labelled; the loading state sets aria-busy and announces "Loading categories".

Decisions to confirm

  • Names are sample data; fallbacks are "Category", "Subcategory" and "Application".
  • Desktop keeps the two-page drill-down in one side sheet instead of two adjacent columns, so the pattern is the same at every width.
  • A failed brand-category request is treated as empty (as the app): no error is shown.
