LOGIN

Layout

  • 375 — the whole slide fits the screen with no scrolling: 4-segment indicator centred at the top, centred title and description (fixed minimum height so the artwork stays aligned between pages), a phone frame showing the built screen at phone size, fading into the background, full-width LOGIN at the bottom.
  • 768 — the same single column, centred with a capped width; previous / next arrows appear under the artwork; the frame is a tablet showing the same screen at tablet size.
  • 1440 — two columns: indicator, large title, description, arrows and LOGIN on the left; a desktop frame on the right showing the same screen at desktop size.
  • States default, page-2, page-3, page-4 are the four pages. No loading, empty or error state (all content is bundled).

Components used

  • Progress (segmented bar) — active segment inverse, others muted
  • Button filled L (LOGIN) · IconButton glass (previous / next, from 768)
  • Illustration: device frame (.wt-device) around a capture of the built screen for that slide: Home, Explore, Discover, Marketplace. The image matches the device: phone under 768, tablet from 768, desktop from 1200, in the light or dark theme. Captures live in assets/walkthrough/. Web difference: the app bundles its own phone artwork.

Motion

  • Page change (in place, nothing reloads): the title and description slide out toward where you came from and the next ones slide in from the direction of travel, with a fade and a slight blur (out --duration-quick; in --duration-medium, --ease, --distance-large, --blur-medium). The device frame stays still and only the screen inside it fades through, a beat later. The indicator segment moves with --tabs-*.
  • LOGIN press — --press-*; to Sign in — --page-slide-* / --panel-*.

Accessibility

  • Carousel semantics: each page is a slide "Slide N of 4" with its own h1; the copy region is polite-live.
  • Swipe on touch; arrow keys, the arrows and the indicator segments on pointer devices (segments have a 44px hit area). No auto-advance.
  • Illustration is decorative. Focus order: indicator → arrows → LOGIN.
