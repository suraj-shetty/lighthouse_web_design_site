Layout

  • Centred dialog at every width (max 400): title, hint line, the cropped image (circle for profile, 16:9 rounded rectangle for cover), two equal buttons.
  • 375 — pinch to zoom and pan, 1× to 4×.
  • 768 / 1440 — zoom with the wheel or the − / + buttons under the image; the hint line changes to match.
  • Not dismissible by tapping outside. States: profile · cover.

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog (confirm)
  • Button glass M (CANCEL) · Button filled M (OK)
  • IconButton glass (zoom, from 768)

Motion

  • Open / close — --modal-*
  • Button press — --press-*
  • OK closes the dialog, the image shows on Manage Account and the blocking loader S-13-09 runs the upload

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title and described by the hint; focus trapped; initial focus on OK.
  • Image alt is "Selected photo preview"; zoom level is announced politely.
  • Zoom out is disabled at 1×; + and − keys also zoom in build.

Decisions to confirm

  • "Scroll or use the buttons to zoom in or out" is new web copy for non-touch zoom; the app's "Use pinch to zoom in or out" stays on mobile.
  • Zoom buttons and the 1× readout are a web adaptation of pinch.
  • No error state (not handled in the app).
