Preview Image

Layout

  • Centred dialog at every width: title, the cropped image, two equal buttons in a row.
  • Not dismissible by tapping outside (as the app).
  • Single state.

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog (confirm)
  • Button glass M ("CANCEL") · Button filled M ("ADD")

Motion

  • Open / close — --modal-*.
  • Button press — --press-*.

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title; focus trapped and returned to "Browse file".
  • Esc acts as CANCEL.
  • The image has a text alternative.

Decisions to confirm

  • "ADD" attaches the image to the draft in S-13-21; "CANCEL" discards it.
  • The sample photo stands in for a scanned certificate.
