Layout

  • 375 — content-height glass sheet: handle → title → subtitle → calendar card (previous · "Month YYYY" · next; 7 columns × 6 weeks, Sunday first). No confirm button — tapping a day picks it and closes.
  • 768 / 1440 — popover anchored to the row that opened it.
  • Canonical date picker. States are its callers: Project Start Date (default; days after the end date are off), Project End Date (days before the start date are off), and the two Submit-quote uses from S-19-05 — Delivery Timeline and Quote Validity (days before today, 13 Nov 2026, are off).
  • Opens on the month of the saved date or the current month; the saved date is filled. Leading and trailing days of adjacent months are shown and selectable when allowed. Loading, empty and error do not apply.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / popover
  • Calendar card · IconButton glass (month navigation) · day cell (default, other month, disabled, selected)

Motion

  • Open / close — --panel-*
  • Month change — --tab-panel-*; day press — --press-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog labelled by its title. Weekday column headers are added for web.
  • Build as a grid: arrow keys move by day / week, Page Up / Down by month; disabled days are exposed as disabled; the selected day is announced.
  • The month heading is a polite live region. The form shows dd-MM-yyyy.

Decisions to confirm

  • Weekday headers are a web addition (the app shows none).
  • A set date cannot be cleared, as in the app.
  • Mockup: previous / next step through three months; in the product navigation is unlimited.
