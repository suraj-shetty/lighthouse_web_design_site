Layout

  • 375 — as the app: hero, product header, QUOTE DETAILS, YOUR REQUEST, DELIVERY LOCATION, SELECTED DEALER (or DEALERS decline cards), proof line and grid, YOUR REVIEW; bottom bar on completed orders only.
  • 768 — one centred column; paired cards side by side; proof grid 4 to 5 columns.
  • 1440 — order content on the left; sticky summary on the right with status, timeline and the actions.
  • States: default completed, review requested (ADD REVIEW filled, download secondary) · reviewed (YOUR REVIEW, download primary) · cancelled · declined · no-proof · downloading · submitting (dimmed spinner overlay) · loading · error.
  • Cancelled and Declined have no proof section and no actions. Cancelled here has no accepted quote, so quote and dealer cards are hidden.

Components used

  • Navigation shell (shared) · Top header (glass, back + title + order number)
  • Tag · Status pill · Card with key / value list · Dealer tile · Metric cells · Decline cards
  • Image grid to gallery viewer (S-05-12) · Rating (read-only stars) · Tag (review tags)
  • Sticky CTA bar · Button filled L · Button translucent L · Steps (1440 only) · Skeleton · Empty line · Toast · Blocking spinner overlay

Motion

  • Arrive from the list / leave — --page-slide-* under 768, --panel-* from 768
  • Skeleton to content — --skel-*, --shimmer-*, --reveal-*; blocks rise with --stagger-*
  • Button and card press — --press-*; toast — --toast-*
  • Review sheet — --panel-*; "YOUR REVIEW" appearing after submit — --reveal-*

Accessibility

  • h1 is the header title; decline cards are list items with the dealer name as heading.
  • Stars are exposed as "Rated 5 out of 5"; review tags are a list.
  • While a review is submitting the overlay is a status region ("Submitting review").
  • Cancelled and Declined have no actions; Back stays reachable in the header.

Decisions to confirm

  • On a completed order the dealer's real name and address are shown, as the app.
  • Invoice download is a browser download ending in the toast from S-11-20; "Open Folder" is dropped.
  • A cancelled order that did have an accepted quote would keep "Authorized Dealer 1"; that variant is not drawn separately.
  • The order-progress timeline in the 1440 summary card is a web addition built from statusHistory; the app shows only the status text. It is not shown under 1200.
  • Under 768 the glass tab bar is hidden on this pushed screen, as in the app, so the sticky action bar owns the bottom edge.
