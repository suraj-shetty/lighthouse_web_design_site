Layout

  • 375 — glass bottom sheet (up to 90% height): handle, title and subtitle, content, full-width button pinned above the safe area.
  • 768 / 1440 — side sheet from the right over Manage Account, same content, button in the sheet footer.
  • Four option pills, radio dot on the right; the saved value is preselected.
  • Error state: the sheet has already closed — only the toast shows over Manage Account, and the row keeps its old value.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / side sheet · IconButton glass (close)
  • Option pill with Radio (Select card pattern)
  • Button filled L, with loading spinner
  • Toast (danger)

Motion

  • Open / close — --panel-* (slides up under 768, slides in from the right from 768).
  • Select — --select-check-*; button press — --press-*; toast — --toast-*.

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by the sheet title; focus moves into the sheet, is trapped, and returns to the row it was opened from; Esc and the scrim close it.
  • Radio group named "Team Size"; arrow keys move the selection.
  • While saving the options are disabled and the button announces "Saving".
  • The error toast is announced through a polite live region.
