Layout

  • Web replaces the app's bottom sheet with a toast at every width: the browser saves the files and manages its own downloads list, so there is no folder to open.
  • 375 — toast at the top. 768 / 1440 — toast at the bottom right. It leaves after 3 seconds.
  • States: default "Saved 2 invoices." · single "Saved 1 invoice." · failed "Download Failed" · no-files "Invoice / No invoice files available to download."
  • Canonical for S-10-18 as well. Screens trigger it with the toast attribute; opened as a layer, a click anywhere dismisses it.

Components used

  • Toast success (completion) · Toast danger (failure, no files)

Motion

  • Toast in / out — --toast-*

Accessibility

  • The toast is a polite live region, so completion is announced ("Download complete. Saved 2 invoices.").
  • Focus stays on the download button; nothing is trapped.
