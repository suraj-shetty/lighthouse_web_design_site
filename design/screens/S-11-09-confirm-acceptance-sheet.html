Confirm Acceptance

Are you sure you want to accept this delivery? Once confirmed, the Order status will be updated to Completed and this action cannot be undone.

ACCEPT
Error

Something went wrong. Please try again later.

Layout

  • 375 — content-height glass bottom sheet: handle, title, body, "ACCEPT" filled above "CANCEL" outlined.
  • 768 / 1440 — centred confirm dialog; CANCEL on the left, ACCEPT on the right.
  • States: default · loading (ACCEPT disabled with spinner) · error (toast, stays open).

Components used

  • Bottom sheet under 768 · Modal / Dialog from 768
  • Button filled L with loading (ACCEPT) · Button translucent L (CANCEL) · Toast danger

Motion

  • Under 768 — --panel-*; from 768 — --modal-*
  • On success, push Delivery Accepted (S-11-18) — --page-slide-*; press — --press-*

Accessibility

  • alertdialog named by the title, described by the body; focus is trapped.
  • Initial focus is on "CANCEL" because the action cannot be undone; focus returns to the ACCEPT trigger on close.
  • Busy state is announced on the ACCEPT button.

Decisions to confirm

  • Sheet becomes a dialog from 768, per the mapping in the spec.
  • Drag down, outside tap, Esc and CANCEL all close without accepting.
