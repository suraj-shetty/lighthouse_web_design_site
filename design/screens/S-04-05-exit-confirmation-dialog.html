Exit Onboarding

Your onboarding progress will be lost. Do you want to exit?

Yes

Layout

  • Centred dialog at every width (fixed maximum width): title, message, two equal buttons side by side — "No" left, "Yes" right.
  • Only the Exit Onboarding variant exists on web; the "Exit App" variant is dropped (no app to close).
  • Not dismissible by tapping outside (as the app). One state; no loading indicator after "Yes" (as the app).

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog (confirm)
  • Button translucent M ("No") · Button filled M ("Yes")

Motion

  • Open / close — --modal-* (scale + fade); press — --press-*.
  • "Yes" ends the session and replaces everything with Sign in — no transition in the app; --panel-* crossfade on web.

Accessibility

  • alertdialog named by its title and described by its message; focus is trapped; initial focus on "No".
  • Esc behaves as "No"; focus is restored to the back button.

Decisions to confirm

  • "Yes" signs the user out (clears stored user data) and returns to Sign in — confirm this is still wanted on web, where a browser back would otherwise just leave the page.
  • The spec names --radius-12 for the surface; the shared dialog shape (--radius-28) is used for consistency with every other dialog.
