Update Photo

Update your profile or cover photo.

Profile

Layout

  • 375 — bottom sheet sized to content: drag handle; centred title and subtitle; a non-interactive "Profile" pill; two side-by-side action boxes (Camera, Gallery); full-width REMOVE PICTURE.
  • 768 / 1440 — small popover anchored to the "+" badge of the photo, same content.
  • One state only. REMOVE PICTURE is shown even when no photo is chosen (as the app). No loading, empty or error state.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / popover
  • Select card (action boxes with icon)
  • Pill (static label)
  • Button translucent L (REMOVE PICTURE)

Motion

  • Open / close — --panel-*; press — --press-*. Every action closes the sheet.

Accessibility

  • Dialog named "Update Photo", described by its subtitle; the three actions are buttons; the pill is plain text.
  • Focus is trapped and returns to the photo control; Esc, scrim and drag down close.
