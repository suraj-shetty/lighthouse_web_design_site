Layout

  • 375 — glass bottom sheet up to 90% height: handle, title and subtitle, two scrolling cards (Product Review, Dealer Review: image, name, rate label, 5 stars, tag chips, comments), pinned "SUBMIT" and "SKIP FOR NOW".
  • 768 / 1440 — side sheet from the right, 440 wide, same stack; the footer stays pinned.
  • States: default all ratings 0, SUBMIT disabled · filled both rated, SUBMIT enabled. The rules run live: tap stars and tags.
  • There is no loading or error state in the sheet: it closes on submit and the parent screen (S-11-11) shows the overlay spinner, then "YOUR REVIEW".

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / side sheet · IconButton glass (close)
  • Section card · Thumbnail / circular dealer tile · Rating (interactive stars)
  • Chip, toggle (default / selected) · Textarea
  • Button filled L (disabled) · Button text

Motion

  • Open / close — --panel-* (bottom sheet under 768, side sheet from 768)
  • Button press — --press-*; toast — --toast-*
  • Star select — --icon-swap-*; tag toggle — --tabs-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by the title; each section is a labelled group with a heading.
  • Stars are a radiogroup ("Rate the product, 1–5 stars") with 44 px targets; a rating cannot return to 0.
  • Tags are toggle buttons with a pressed state; once 3 are selected the rest stop responding and dim, and the group label carries "Choose up to 3".
  • SUBMIT explains why it is disabled to screen readers.

Decisions to confirm

  • Section cards stay stacked in the side sheet rather than side by side.
  • A failed submit loses the entered review, as the app (toast on the parent screen).
  • Dealer name on the review card is the real name, since the order is completed.
