Delete Confirmation

Are you sure you want to delete this post?

Layout

  • Centred dialog at every width, over the options menu. Title, one line of body, two equal buttons.
  • Not dismissible by tapping outside (as the app).
  • Body reads "post" or "project" from the item type.

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog (destructive confirm)
  • Button filled M (Cancel) · Button destructive M (Delete, with loading)
  • Toast (error)

Motion

  • Open / close — --modal-* (scale .96 + fade)
  • Button press — --press-*

Accessibility

  • alertdialog named by its title and described by its body; focus is trapped; initial focus on Cancel.
  • While deleting both buttons are disabled and the Delete button announces "Deleting".
  • Destructive action is separated by colour and label.
