Layout

  • Centred dialog at every width: title → single-line text field (focused on open) → Cancel and Add side by side.
  • Two callers as states: "Unlisted product" (default) and "Custom item"; only the title and the field hint change.
  • Add trims the text and appends a removable chip to the row; an empty entry just closes. Duplicates are allowed. Tapping outside cancels.
  • Loading, empty and error do not apply.

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog
  • Text field · Button stroke M (Cancel) · Button filled M (Add)

Motion

  • Open / close — --modal-*
  • Button press — --press-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog labelled by its title; the field takes its name from the title.
  • Enter submits, Esc cancels; focus is trapped and returns to the row's Add button.

Decisions to confirm

  • Cancel is drawn as a secondary (stroke) button; the app draws both buttons filled.
  • No max length and no validation message, as in the app.
