Layout

  • 375 — glass bottom sheet sized to its content: drag handle, title, subtitle, two stacked full-width buttons "YES" and "NO".
  • 768 / 1440 — side sheet from the right over Manage Account; the two buttons stay directly under the subtitle instead of being pinned to the bottom, so the question and its answers read as one group.
  • default · saving both buttons disabled, spinner in the one that was tapped · server-error.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / side sheet
  • IconButton glass (close)
  • Button filled L (YES) · Button translucent L (NO)
  • Toast danger

Motion

  • Open / close — --panel-*
  • Button press — --press-*
  • Error toast — --toast-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title; focus moves into the sheet, is trapped, and returns to the Manage Account row that opened it; Esc or the scrim closes without saving.
  • While saving the primary button is disabled and announces its busy label; toasts are announced through a polite live region.
  • Both buttons are disabled while the request runs; the tapped one announces "Saving".

Decisions to confirm

  • Brand only. The sheet gives no indication of the saved value (as the app) — a Yes / No segmented toggle showing it would be clearer; confirm before changing.
  • Subtitle "Choose supports project based pricing." kept verbatim.
  • Tapping the already-saved answer closes without a request. Resellers set this with a switch on Manage Account (S-13-05), not this sheet.
  • State server-error shows the sheet as it stays after a failed request, with the "Error" toast (server message, else "Network error occurred" / "Something went wrong. Please try again later."). No retry control, as the app.
