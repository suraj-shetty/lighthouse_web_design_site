Delete Account

Are you sure you want to delete your account? Deleting your account is permanent. All your data will be removed and cannot be recovered.

What will happen when you delete account

  • Your profile and all data will be permanently deleted.
  • You won’t be able to reactivate or retrieve any of the content you have added.

Layout

  • 375: full screen, as the app: back (left), title, close X (right); warning paragraph, sub-heading, two bullet rows with arrow icons; button pinned at the bottom.
  • 768 / 1440: centred dialog (480 wide) over the reason screen instead of a second page; same copy; one close button.
  • Back returns to the reason screen. Close X on mobile leaves both delete screens and returns to Manage Account.
  • On success the session ends on the device and on the server, and Sign in (S-02-01) is shown.

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog (destructive confirm), full screen under 768
  • IconButton plain (back) · IconButton glass (close)
  • Button destructive L (with loading) · Toast danger

Motion

  • Open / close: --page-slide-* under 768, --modal-* from 768
  • Button press: --press-*; toast, --toast-*
  • After success: no transition (as the app).

Accessibility

  • alertdialog named "Delete Account", described by the warning paragraph; focus trapped; Esc cancels; first focus is not the destructive button.
  • The button states its consequence in its name; while deleting it is disabled and announces "Deleting your account".
  • Bullet arrows are decorative.

Decisions to confirm

  • Open (Q-120): the app styles this button with the primary colours. The spec lists the destructive variant, used here: confirm.
  • No typed confirmation or re-authentication step exists in the app.
  • Open (Q-122): whether the spinner shows in the app is unverified; the web shows it.
