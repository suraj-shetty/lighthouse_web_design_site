Layout

  • 375: mirrors the app: centred title with back, intro paragraph, one card of radio rows, "CONTINUE" pinned at the bottom. No tab bar (pushed screen).
  • 768: same column, reading width 640, glass menu on the left; the button sits right-aligned in the sticky bar.
  • 1440: same column beside the expanded menu. "CONTINUE" opens the confirmation (S-15-05) as a centred dialog over this page.
  • Empty and error show only "No reasons available.": no button, as the app.

Components used

  • Top header (back) · Card · Radio rows (single choice)
  • Sticky CTA bar · Button filled L · Skeleton · Toast danger

Motion

  • Route push: --page-slide-* under 768, --panel-* crossfade from 768
  • Radio: --select-check-*; press, --press-*
  • Skeleton to content: --skel-*, --shimmer-*, --reveal-*; confirmation, --modal-*

Accessibility

  • main landmark; "Delete Account" is the h1; back button labelled "Back".
  • Reasons are one radio group labelled by the intro text; arrow keys move the choice.
  • Error toast is a polite live region; every row is at least 56 high.

Decisions to confirm

  • Open (Q-120): the app lets the user continue with no reason (it would send "null"). This design follows the app; requiring a reason is recommended.
  • Intro and reason labels come from the API. The intro shown is the fallback text in the app code; the six reasons are sample data.
  • Error has no retry control in the app.
