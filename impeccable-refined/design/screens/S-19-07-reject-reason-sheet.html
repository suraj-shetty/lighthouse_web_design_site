Layout

  • 375: glass bottom sheet sized to its content: drag handle, title, six radio rows, full-width "Save".
  • 768 / 1440: the same list as a menu anchored to the reason field on Reject order.
  • "default": nothing chosen and "Save" disabled. "selected": the reason already on the form is pre-selected.
  • Choosing an option keeps the sheet open; "Save" closes it and fills the field. Scrim, Esc or drag down closes without changing the form.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / popover menu · Radio rows (6) · Button filled L (Save, disabled)

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-* (sheet slides up on mobile; menu fades and scales from the field from 768)
  • Radio: --select-check-*; button press, --press-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title; options are one radio group with arrow-key navigation.
  • The disabled state of "Save" is exposed; Esc closes without change; focus returns to the reason field.

Decisions to confirm

  • In the mockup "Save" returns to Reject order in its "selected" state, which always shows "Cannot meet the required timeline".
  • Choosing "Other" does not make the note required.
