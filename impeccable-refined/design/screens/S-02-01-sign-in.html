Layout

  • 375: as the app: no header bar; centred title; one pill field (flag · chevron · dial code, then the number); centred helper; terms sentence and full-width CONTINUE pinned to the bottom.
  • 768: the same content in a centred card; CONTINUE sits directly under the terms sentence.
  • 1440: the card on the right, brand artwork with the tagline on the left.
  • States: default (empty, muted button), valid (10 digits, active button), invalid (inline error), loading (spinner in the button, field stays editable), error (toast "Error" + message), debug (non-production "Debug" label). No empty state.

Components used

  • Text field with a leading country Select trigger (opens S-02-02)
  • Button filled L: active, muted-but-tappable, loading
  • Text links (Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy)
  • Toast danger

Motion

  • Country picker: --panel-*; button press, --press-*; toast, --toast-*.
  • To Verify Your Number: --page-slide-* under 768, --panel-* from 768.

Accessibility

  • h1 = title. The field has the programmatic label "Mobile number", inputmode=numeric, autocomplete=tel-national, max 10 digits.
  • Country trigger is a button named "India, +91. Change country".
  • Inline error is tied to the field and announced; the toast is announced.
  • CONTINUE in its muted style stays focusable and tappable (it runs validation), as in the app. Focus order: country → number → terms links → CONTINUE.

Decisions to confirm

  • Q-02 applied: the session persists across visits and tabs until sign-out: no "remember me" control is shown.
  • The OTP is requested automatically on the 10th valid digit (as the app); CONTINUE remains for keyboard users.
  • Q-43: number length is fixed at 10 digits for every country.
  • The "Exit App" dialog on system back is dropped on web.
  • Desktop artwork reuses the splash tagline; final imagery to be supplied.
  • The app has no visible field label; "Mobile number" is screen-reader only.
