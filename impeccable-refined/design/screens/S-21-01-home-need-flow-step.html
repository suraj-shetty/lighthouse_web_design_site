Layout

  • 375: mirrors NeedFlowScreen: back button at the left, segmented progress centred on the screen, the two-line context line from step 2, then title, subtitle and the step body. Footer buttons are stacked full width, 8 apart. No bottom tab bar (pushed screen).
  • 768 / 1440: one centred column capped at 600 inside the app frame. Header, question and footer share that column. Building-type and who grids go to 4 columns; the repair grid stays at 3 because it has 6 tiles. Footer buttons sit side by side, right-aligned under the column, 280 wide at most. "Step 2 of 4" is shown as text at the right of the header bar.
  • Step bodies: photo tile grid (default, repair, who, explore-type), select rows single (subtype, repair-help, repair-when, stage, budget, start) and multi (pros, spaces), grouped rows (repair-choose), select pills single (who-help) and multi (help, assist-help), location (location, empty), details (repair-details, repair-details-photos, pro-details).
  • Demo walk, flow A: default, subtype, location, help, help-selected, pros, pros-selected, then S-21-03. Repair: repair, repair-when (or repair-help / repair-choose first), repair-details, repair-details-photos, then S-21-03. Also wired: who, who-help, pro-details; explore-type, explore-kind; assist-notice, assist-help; stage, budget, start; send-budget, send-start.
  • Title-only variants not drawn as separate states: "What are you renovating?" with "Homes, workplaces, shops, hotels and more. Pick the closest." (renovation), "What kind of property is it?" (Get started), and the renovation note on Help me choose: "We pick from your property and spaces, and say who."

Components used

  • From lh.css: glass header panel, IconButton (glass, back), Button filled L, Button stroke L (SKIP, BROWSE ALL PROJECTS), Button filled S (Add photo, Retry as stroke S), Select (location pill), Textarea, Chip (budget), Info banner (notice), Skeleton, Toast, sticky footer CTA bar.
  • New in f-21.css: segmented progress .nf-prog, context line .nf-ctx, photo tile .nf-tile (single photo and 2 x 2 mosaic, selected check), check disc .nf-disc (radio dot and tick), select row .nf-row with group heading, select pill .nf-pill, photo box and thumbnails .nf-photo / .nf-thumbs, inline error row .nf-err, footer .nf-foot.
  • States drawn per component: rest, hover (halo on the disc, lifted shadow on the tile), focus (the kit ring), pressed, selected, disabled CTA. All colours are tokens, so dark is the token swap.

Motion

  • Step change (Flutter 340 ms easeInOutCubic, in from 100% right, out to minus 25%): --page-slide-* on the question region only. The mockup reloads per state, so it plays the entrance over --distance-large; the build should use --page-slide-distance and --page-parallax, mirrored on Back.
  • Context line (Flutter 220 ms in, 140 ms out, easeOutCubic; header height 240 ms): --text-swap-*, header height on --resize-*.
  • Check disc (fill 150 ms, mark after 80 ms over 250 ms, clear 150 ms): --select-check-fill, --select-check-delay, --select-check-mark, --select-check-clear; dot on --select-check-dot-ease, tick on --select-check-ease. Budget chips: --select-check-fill.
  • Progress segment colour (Flutter 200 ms): --duration-fast with --ease. Press (Flutter 0.97, 150 ms): --press-*. Skeleton: --shimmer-*. Sheet: --panel-*. Toast: --toast-*.
  • Reduced motion: every duration token is zero, so steps and marks change without movement.

Accessibility

  • One main landmark; the step title is the h1 and takes focus on each step change. "Step n of total" is a polite status: visible from 768, screen-reader text under 768. The segments are decorative.
  • Tile grids, single rows and single pills are radio groups named by the title; multi rows and pills are checkbox groups. Selection happens on click, Enter or Space, never on arrow focus, because single-select moves on at once.
  • Selected state is never colour alone: the disc fills and shows a dot or a tick; a chosen tile shows a check disc on its photo.
  • Rows are 56 high, pills 54, tiles and chips have 44 targets, the 22 remove-photo button has a 44 hit area and the label "Remove photo n".
  • The location pill is a button named "Location: value" that opens a dialog. "(Optional)" is inside each field label. A disabled CTA uses aria-disabled and stays in the tab order. Footer height is reserved so focused rows are not covered.

Decisions to confirm

  • Tiles show a check when selected. The app draws no selected state on tiles; the check is only seen when the user comes back to the step.
  • Selected pill surface (app: raw grey) uses --element-bg-select-card-card-default; the notice banner (app: lavender gradient) uses the kit info banner on --accent-tonal.
  • Footer is solid from 16 down with a short fade and --blur-footer; the app lets content show through a 60% to 92% gradient.
  • "What is going wrong?" rows print the trade key in lower case under each label, as the app does.
  • Location is optional and NEXT is always enabled, as the app. "Add photo" becomes a browser file input; nothing enforces "JPG or PNG".
  • Budget chips are toggles here; in the build only one can be on and tapping it again clears it.
  • The help step shows five progress segments before and after a choice, so the bar does not jump when the first option is picked (the app adds the fifth segment at that moment).
  • In the demo, multi-select CTAs enable through the first option only (help to help-selected, pros to pros-selected); spaces rejoins flow A at the location step; assist-notice jumps to assist-help; save-subtype shows the one-question case, so it has no progress bar.
  • The error state (no building types at all) cannot occur with the built-in list; its message is sample text.
