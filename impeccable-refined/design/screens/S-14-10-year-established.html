Layout

  • 375: glass bottom sheet over the scrim: drag handle, title, subtitle, one centred stepper: minus, year, plus, "Update".
  • 768 / 1440: side sheet from the right over Manage Account (440 wide, no drag handle); same order, actions pinned to the bottom.
  • default saved year · unset nothing saved, so the current year (2026) is shown and plus is disabled at the upper bound · minimum 1900, minus disabled · saving · server-error.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / side sheet
  • IconButton glass (close)
  • Qty stepper (large, min / max, disabled)
  • Button filled L (Update, with loading)
  • Toast danger

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-*
  • Button press: --press-*
  • Error toast: --toast-*
  • Year change: --text-swap-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title; focus moves into the sheet, is trapped, and returns to the Manage Account row that opened it; Esc or the scrim closes without saving.
  • While saving the primary button is disabled and announces its busy label; toasts are announced through a polite live region.
  • Stepper buttons are labelled "Earlier year" / "Later year"; the button at a bound is disabled.
  • The year is a labelled numeric input, so it can also be typed.

Decisions to confirm

  • Typed year is a web adaptation (the app only steps one year at a time). Bounds 1900 to the current year apply; the app has no copy for an out-of-range typed value, so none is drawn: needs a message if typing is approved.
  • Subtitle says "your brand" for resellers too (Q-118): kept verbatim.
  • "Update" always sends the request, even when the year is unchanged (as the app).
  • State server-error shows the sheet as it stays after a failed request, with the "Error" toast (server message, else "Network error occurred" / "Something went wrong. Please try again later."). No retry control, as the app.
