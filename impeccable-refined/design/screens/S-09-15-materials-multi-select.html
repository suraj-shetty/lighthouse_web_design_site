Layout

  • 375: glass sheet (opens at 80%): handle → title → subtitle → scrolling checkbox rows → full-width DONE.
  • 768 / 1440: side sheet, same order.
  • Three callers as states: Material Categories (default), Subcategories (children of the chosen categories) and Brands. No limit; DONE is always enabled and an empty selection clears the field.
  • Empty shows "Nothing to pick here yet". Error is the Brands case: the sheet opens empty with a toast. There is no loading state here: options are fetched before the sheet opens.

Components used

  • Side / bottom sheet · IconButton glass (close)
  • Checkbox rows · Button filled L (DONE) · Toast

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-*
  • Checkbox: --select-check-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog labelled by its title and described by the subtitle; options are a checkbox group.
  • Focus is trapped and returns to the row that opened the sheet.
