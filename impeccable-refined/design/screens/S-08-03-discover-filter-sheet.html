Layout

  • 375: glass bottom sheet (up to 90% height): drag handle; header with filter icon, title, close (×) and helper line; divider; two panes, role categories on the left (the same as the Discover tabs, the active one raised with a chevron), options on the right with a check column and "All" first; RESET and APPLY side by side at the bottom.
  • 768 / 1440: side sheet from the right with the same two panes; actions pinned at the bottom.
  • Tap a category to load its options (try it in Default). The filter is per category: changing category clears the in-sheet selection. "All" is checked when nothing is selected and clears the others.
  • APPLY shows the count when options are selected: "APPLY (2)". Applying switches the Discover tab to the active category if it differs.
  • Loading: skeleton rows in both panes until categories arrive; skeleton rows in the right pane while a category's options load. Empty and error: the right pane shows only "All" (error adds the toast).

Components used

  • Side sheet / Bottom sheet
  • IconButton glass (close)
  • Two-pane picker: vertical category tabs + checkbox option rows
  • Button stroke L (RESET) · Button filled L (APPLY / APPLY (n))
  • Skeleton · Toast

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-*
  • Category change: --tab-panel-*
  • Option check: --select-check-*
  • Button press: --press-*; skeleton, --shimmer-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog labelled "Filters", described by the helper line; focus trapped and returned to the filter button; Esc closes without applying.
  • Categories are a vertical tablist controlling the options panel; options are checkboxes with visible labels; "All" acts as clear-all.
  • APPLY's label includes the count; the active category is marked by the raised fill, weight and chevron, not colour alone.

Decisions to confirm

  • RESET uses the stroke button to match the feed filter sheet (S-05-02); the spec names the translucent button.
  • Category and option names are sample data; the real ones come from the API.
  • In the mockup RESET, the "All" rule and the live count are not wired; category switching and option checks are.
