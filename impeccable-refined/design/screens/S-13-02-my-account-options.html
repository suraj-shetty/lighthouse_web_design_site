Layout

  • 375: glass bottom sheet sized to its content: drag handle, title, subtitle, three rows divided by hairlines. The first two rows end in a round glass arrow; Logout has none.
  • 768 / 1440: the same content as a 320 wide menu anchored under the More button of My profile; no scrim tint.
  • One state only (the app's sheet is static).

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / popover menu
  • Menu row (leading icon, label, trailing round arrow)
  • IconButton glass (row arrow, decorative)

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-* (slides up on mobile; fades and scales from the trigger from 768)
  • Row press: --press-*
  • Manage Account / Manage Notification close the menu and change route: --page-slide-*; Logout opens S-13-03 on top, --modal-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title "My Account"; focus moves to the first row, is trapped, and returns to More on close.
  • Esc, scrim tap and (mobile) drag down close it.
  • Rows are links (navigation) or a button (Logout), each with a text label; arrows are decorative.
