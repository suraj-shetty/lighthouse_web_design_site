Paints & Finishes

Layout

  • 375: full-screen glass layer over the Marketplace home: title and subtitle top-left, round close top-right, rows (icon, name, chevron chip) with hairlines.
  • 768 / 1440: side sheet from the right with the same list (up to 500 categories scroll inside).
  • Empty: header only, as the app (no empty text exists).

Components used

  • Full-screen layer / side sheet · IconButton glass (close)
  • Menu row with leading glyph and trailing glass chevron chip

Motion

  • Open / close: --modal-* fade under 768, --panel-* from 768
  • Row press: --press-*; a row closes this layer, then opens S-10-04 (--panel-*) or pushes S-10-07

Accessibility

  • Dialog named "Categories"; focus trapped and returned to the "View All Categories" tile; Esc closes.
  • Rows are buttons (or links when the category has no subcategories); glyphs and chevrons are decorative.
  • Close button is labelled "Close".

Decisions to confirm

  • Category names are sample data. Glyphs cycle through the app's five fixed icons by index.
  • No loading or error state: the list is passed in already loaded.
