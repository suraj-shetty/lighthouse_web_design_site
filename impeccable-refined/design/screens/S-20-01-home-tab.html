Layout

  • 375: as the app: avatar, wordmark and bell on row 1, city button on row 2; hero, search pill, "START WITH WHAT YOU NEED" with the intent grid 3 across, Your requests, offer card, Suggestions; glass tab bar with Home active.
  • 768: one centred column. Intent tiles 6 across at phone size, request and professional cards 2 up, project row scrolls. Avatar, wordmark and bell move to the menu, so the header holds the city button and Refresh.
  • 1024 and up: two columns: the needs (hero, search, intents) stay on the left and stick; requests, the offer and suggestions scroll on the right in the app's order. With nothing to show on the right (empty state) the page stays one column.
  • States: default no requests; with-requests draft, browsing and sent cards; with-suggestions professionals for the newest request; with-projects projects when the newest request is a renovation; loading, empty and error per section; profile-incomplete looks like default but the offer button opens S-20-03.
  • Empty: city reads "Select city", the grid, offer, requests and suggestions are hidden and the section label stays, as in the app. There is no whole-page empty state.

Components used

  • Navigation shell (five tabs), Top header (glass on scroll), Avatar, IconButton with unread dot, Location selector
  • New in f-20.css: search pill (button with rotating examples), intent tile, offer card, section header with action, request card, professional card, project card and row, inline error row
  • Button filled and stroke (M, S), Rating, Verified badge, Skeleton, Toast

Motion

  • Search example swap: --text-swap-* with --search-example-hold between words (the app holds 2.5 s and fades for 150 ms; nearest tokens). No swap under reduced motion.
  • Tiles, pills and cards press: --press-* (the app scales to 0.97 over 150 ms).
  • Sections: --skel-*, --shimmer-*, --reveal-*; page entrance --stagger-*; sheets --panel-*; pushes --page-slide-*; toast --toast-*.

Accessibility

  • h1 is the hero title. The uppercase section labels are the h2 and h3 headings; card titles sit below them.
  • The search pill is a link named "Search"; the rotating word is hidden from assistive technology.
  • City button: "Location: Bengaluru. Change city". Bell: "Notifications, unread". Request buttons carry the request title ("Continue: Residential"); each View All names its section.
  • Avatar and bell are drawn at the app's size inside 44px targets. Error rows are polite status regions; skeleton groups are marked busy.

Decisions to confirm

  • From 1024 the offer sits in the right column under Your requests, keeping the app's order, so the left column is never beside an empty space. The prompt asked for the offer on the left; say if you prefer that.
  • Search pill border and glow use --accent, --palette-purple-700 and --palette-pink-700 (600 in dark): no gradient token exists (Q-161).
  • Glyphs not approved: FileEdit drawn as File02, ViewOff as Lock, Sent as Navigation03 (Q-162).
  • The bell is dropped from 768 because Notifications is in the menu (Q-165). The search pill opens Discover, as in the app (Q-163).
  • Request, professional and project data are samples. The suggestions error text is whatever the server sends; the line shown is a stand-in.
