Layout

  • 375: glass bottom sheet: title, subtitle, close, category rows (icon, name, chevron chip).
  • 768 / 1440: dropdown popover under the list title selector; the list scrolls inside.
  • Empty: header only, as the app.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / popover · IconButton glass (close)
  • Menu row with leading glyph and trailing chevron chip; current category marked

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-* (sheet slides up; popover fades and scales from the title)
  • Row press: --press-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named "Categories"; focus trapped and returned to the title selector; Esc closes with no change.
  • Rows are links; the current category carries aria-current and a tinted row (the app shows no selected indicator).

Decisions to confirm

  • Picking a category selects its first subcategory, scrolls to top and resets filters and sort: there is no subcategory picker here.
  • Marking the current category is a web addition for accessibility.
  • Opened from a brand, the rows are that brand's categories.
