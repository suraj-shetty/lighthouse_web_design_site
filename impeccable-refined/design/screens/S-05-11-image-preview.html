Layout

  • 375: full screen on the page background: glass gradient header (round back button, title up to 2 lines) over a single image fitted (contain) to the remaining area.
  • 768: large modal, image centred, title and close (×) in the header.
  • 1440: lightbox modal (up to 1120 × 820) with title, close and a zoom toggle bottom-right; the image is centred, never stretched.
  • Shared widget: opened from an order's invoice tile and from a certificate file, never from the feed. The title comes from the caller (here the product title of the order).

Components used

Motion

Accessibility

  • The title is the dialog heading; the image alt text is the title.
