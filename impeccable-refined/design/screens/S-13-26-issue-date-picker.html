Layout

  • 375: centred dialog: selected date, typed-entry field, month bar (month / year switch, previous, next), weekday row, day grid, Cancel / OK.
  • 768 / 1440: the same calendar as a popover anchored to the Issue Date row of S-13-21.
  • States: default opens on the current issue date (Jan 5, 2025) · today no date yet, opens on today (13 Nov 2026, ringed) · years year switch.
  • Selectable range: 1 Jan of (this year − 100) to 31 Dec of (this year + 2).

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog under 768, popover from 768
  • Calendar grid (new component, Q-14) · Text field (typed date)
  • IconButton plain (previous / next) · Button stroke M · Button filled M

Motion

  • Open / close: --modal-* under 768, --panel-* pop from 768.
  • Day select: --select-check-*; press, --press-*.

Accessibility

  • Days are reachable with arrow keys; month and year controls are labelled; the selected date is announced (live heading).
  • Today is marked with a ring and aria-current="date"; the selected day with fill and checked state: not colour alone.
  • A date can also be typed (DD/MM/YYYY).
  • Build note: use the ARIA grid pattern for the day grid; the mockup uses a radio group so selection works without script.

Decisions to confirm

  • Q-115: all labels here ("Select date", "Cancel", "OK", "DD/MM/YYYY") are web copy; the app shows the platform date picker, so none is defined in code.
  • Q-110: the picker allows dates up to two years in the future. Intended for an issue date?
  • Selected day uses --accent, as the spec tokens say.
  • The value is written back to the form as "Mon D, YYYY" and clears "Issue date is required".
