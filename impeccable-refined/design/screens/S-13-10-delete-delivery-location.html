Delete Location

Are you sure you want to delete this delivery location?

Layout

  • Centred dialog at every width (max 400): title, one line of body, Cancel and a red Delete side by side.
  • Opened from the trash button of a row in DELIVERY LOCATIONS on Manage Account.
  • One state: after Delete the dialog closes and that row's trash becomes a small spinner (drawn as the "deleting" state of S-13-05).

Components used

  • Modal / Dialog (destructive confirm)
  • Button filled M (Cancel) · Button destructive M (Delete)

Motion

  • Open / close: --modal-*
  • Button press: --press-*

Accessibility

  • alertdialog named by its title and described by its body; focus trapped; initial focus on Cancel; Esc = Cancel.
  • Delete is marked by its label as well as colour; focus returns to the delivery list on close.

Decisions to confirm

  • Buttons are drawn at size M to match the other confirm dialogs (spec says S).
  • Delete errors are handled by the delivery-location feature (F-14).
  • The dialog can be dismissed by tapping outside, as the app's default dialog.
