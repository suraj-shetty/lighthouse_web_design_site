Layout

  • 375: glass bottom sheet (up to 90% height): handle, title and subtitle, content, full-width button pinned above the safe area.
  • 768 / 1440: side sheet from the right over Manage Account, same content, button in the sheet footer.
  • "Currency" selector pill (symbol and code, chevron) with its helper line, then "Amount" with the currency symbol as prefix and the inline error line.
  • States: default saved INR 500000 · empty hint "0" · zero and too-large live validation, button disabled · loading saving · error toast, sheet stays open.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / side sheet · IconButton glass (close)
  • Select (opens S-13-29) · Text field with prefix
  • Helper and error text
  • Button filled L, with loading and disabled · Toast (danger)

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-* (slides up under 768, slides in from the right from 768).
  • Currency list: --modal-*; press, --press-*; toast, --toast-*.

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by the sheet title; focus moves into the sheet, is trapped, and returns to the row it was opened from; Esc and the scrim close it.
  • Currency is a labelled button that opens a listbox; Amount has inputmode="numeric", its label, and its error linked and announced.
  • The error is text plus a red border, not colour alone. The disabled button state is exposed.

Decisions to confirm

  • Third live message, not drawn as its own state: "Please enter a valid budget value" (shown when the text is not a number; the field only accepts digits, max 15).
  • Q-113: an empty amount blocks the save silently: "Please enter an amount" is returned by the app but never shown. Show it on web?
  • The helper "Choose the currency you want to quote" sits under the currency field with an info icon (verified in the app source).
  • The amount is shown as typed, without digit grouping (the app saves and shows "INR 500000"). Group as 5,00,000 on web?
  • The subtitle says "budget range" although the sheet takes a single amount (verbatim).
