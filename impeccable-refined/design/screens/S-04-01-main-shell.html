Layout

  • 375: as the app: floating glass pill centred at the bottom with five icon-only tabs; a separate circular glass action button to its right when the role has one. Content scrolls behind the bar.
  • 768: floating glass rail on the left: logo mark (expands the menu), five destinations with tooltips, action button, profile at the bottom.
  • 1440: the same menu expanded to a sidebar with labels; it collapses back to the rail and remembers the choice.
  • Roles: professional: Create "+"; reseller, More actions (opens S-04-03); homeowner, no action button.
  • States: default; loading: five tabs and no action button until the profile (role) has loaded, page area shows the tab's own skeleton. Empty and error belong to the tab pages, not the shell.
  • The content area here is a neutral placeholder; every signed-in screen is drawn inside this shell.

Components used

  • Nav bar (mobile bottom tabs: glass, active pill, unread dot)
  • Sidebar / rail (from 768: glass, collapsible, tooltips)
  • IconButton glass (Create / More actions)
  • Avatar (profile link) · Skeleton

Motion

  • Tab change: the active pill stretches to the new item and settles: --tabs-*; page content, --tab-panel-* / staggered rise --stagger-*.
  • Unread dot: --badge-pop-*; rail ⇄ sidebar, --resize-*; press, --press-*.
  • More actions menu: --panel-*; Create, --page-slide-*.

Accessibility

  • nav landmark labelled "Primary"; each destination is a link with a text label (visually hidden on mobile, tooltip on the rail) and aria-current="page" when active.
  • The Notifications label includes "unread" while the dot shows: not colour alone.
  • Action button is labelled "Create" / "More actions"; all targets are at least 44×44.
  • Page bottom padding keeps the last content clear of the floating bar.
