Layout

  • 375: short glass sheet: handle → title → subtitle → stepper (minus · year · plus) → full-width Done.
  • 768 / 1440: popover anchored to the Completion Year row.
  • Opens on the saved year, or the current year (2026). Range is the current year −10 … +5 (2016 – 2031); minus is disabled at the lower end, plus at the upper end.
  • Loading, empty and error do not apply: nothing is fetched.

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / popover
  • Qty stepper (year) · Button filled L (Done)

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-*
  • Year change: --text-swap-*; button press, --press-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog labelled by its title. The year is a spin button with min, max and current value; arrow keys change it.
  • Disabled ends are exposed as disabled; focus returns to the row.

Decisions to confirm

  • Mockup: the stepper value is static; use the min / max states to see the ends.
