Layout

  • 375: glass bottom sheet: handle, title, subtitle, a stepper row (round minus, large two-digit number with "Years" under it, round plus), full-width Update pinned above the safe area.
  • 768 / 1440: 440 wide side sheet from the right with the same content; the primary button sits in the sheet footer.
  • States: default (seeded with the saved value, "12") · empty ("00", minus disabled) · max ("100", plus disabled) · loading (button spinner) · error (toast; the sheet stays open).

Components used

  • Bottom sheet / side sheet · IconButton glass (close, minus, plus)
  • Stepper (Qty stepper pattern at sheet size)
  • Button filled L (Update, loading) · Toast (danger)

Motion

  • Open / close: --panel-* (slides up on mobile, slides in from the right from 768)
  • Button and row press: --press-*
  • Error toast: --toast-*
  • Number change: --text-swap-*

Accessibility

  • Dialog named by its title; focus moves into the sheet, is trapped, and returns to the row that opened it.
  • Esc, the close button, scrim tap and (mobile) drag down close it; the primary button is last in tab order.
  • While saving the button is disabled and announces "Saving"; errors are announced in a polite live region.
  • The number is a spinbutton (0 – 100) named "Years of experience" with value text "12 Years"; arrow keys change it; − / + are labelled Decrease / Increase and disable at the ends.

Decisions to confirm

  • Values under 10 are zero-padded to two digits ("00", "07") as the app.
  • Unlike the other edit sheets this one stays open when the save fails (closes only on success), as the app.
  • Title is "Year of Experience" (singular) while the row reads "Years of Experience": both verbatim.
  • A close button is added in the header (the app closes by drag or tapping outside only).
  • The stepper buttons are static in this mockup (no script).
